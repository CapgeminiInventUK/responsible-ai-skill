# Human decisions and concern lifecycle
Permitted concern states: Open, Mitigation Planned, In Progress, Awaiting Evidence, Verification, Closed, Regression. New concerns start Open. Preserve prior history; record from/to, timestamp, actor type, actor, reason, evidence IDs and decision ID for every change. Model proposals are separate from human decisions and do not enact approval or closure.
Closure requires reviewed supporting evidence and an explicit authorised human Closure decision referencing that concern. Record decision maker, authority as supplied, timestamp, rationale and evidence IDs. Do not claim identity verification. A plan, user silence, an omitted row, a new report or an apparently improved status cannot close a concern. Verification means evidence awaits authorised judgement. Regression requires evidence of recurrence and a recorded reason; uncertain continuity must not overwrite an old concern.
Mandatory/high-impact applicability exclusions require an Applicability exception decision linked to the control. Policy interpretation, Risk acceptance and Hard-gate review decisions must be explicit and traceable. A recorded human decision does not convert the model's assessment into certification.
Open questions use `assets/open-questions.md` and canonical structured records. State Open or Resolved; include materiality, affected scope, blocking flag, requested answer and answer/decision provenance. Resolve only with supplied answers. Stop and ask whenever an ambiguity affects the result. Unknown metadata is not silently resolved by a default. Package state, not conversation memory, is the reusable record.
