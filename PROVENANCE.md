# Provenance and changes
Built 30 October 2026 from the three user-uploaded files:
- `manifest.json`: original Responsible AI Agent packaging metadata. No original app IDs, developer attribution or deployment capability is claimed for this new skill.
- `declarativeAgent_0.json`: Digital Factory Responsible AI assessment and detailed action-plan design; evidence-backed closure and contextual findings retained.
- `AI_DF_Responsible_AI_Assessment_MVP_PRD_v2.0.docx`: governing MVP scope, sections 1-22; version 2.0 draft dated 22 October 2026. No Word comments part was present in the supplied DOCX.

The original source files are not redistributed in this bundle. Their names and relevant design decisions are documented here and in reference 01. Original ISO/ICO/Capgemini references are candidate source context only, not newly verified policy obligations. No external policy research or source refresh was performed.

Main changes: remove weighted scoring, maturity score, trust labels and autonomous assurance decisions; replace connector capabilities with manual authorised evidence; add policy-pack approval blocking, control-level evidence status, fixed executable rules, questions, human provenance, read-only handling, optional supplied-package comparison and consistent downloadable records.

New technical artefacts: JSON schemas, exact-status rule engine, report renderer, ZIP exporter, synthetic unit tests and evaluation checklist. These are implementation choices. Their local tests do not establish real policy correctness or Claude tenant compatibility.
